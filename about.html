WHO WE ARE

Bringing businesses and suppliers closer together.

Stackly is a modern B2B procurement platform designed to make business purchasing simpler, more organized and more connected.

We bring buyers, suppliers, products and purchasing workflows together in one centralized digital environment. From discovering suppliers to managing purchase orders, Stackly helps businesses move beyond disconnected processes and work with greater clarity.
